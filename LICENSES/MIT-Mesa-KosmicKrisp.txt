gl/libvulkan_kosmickrisp.dylib when the build has it. KosmicKrisp is Copyright 2025-2026 LunarG, Inc. and Google LLC, with files
Copyright Collabora Ltd. and Red Hat Inc., Valve Corporation, Alyssa Rosenzweig, Intel Corporation, Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.,
Imagination Technologies Ltd. and Broadcom; the files of src/kosmickrisp carry the MIT licence, by its SPDX identifier or (three
generator scripts) by its full text; one more build-time generator script, util/vk_extensions.py, has no header. Most of Mesa is
under the MIT licence below; each source file's SPDX identifier governs, and the other licences of Mesa's components are named in its
docs/license.rst, which the build copies into the bundle as licenses/KosmicKrisp-Mesa-license.rst. The patches the library is built

licence.
